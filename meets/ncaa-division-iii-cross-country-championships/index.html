NCAA Division III Cross Country Championships

NCAA Division III Cross Country Championships · Northfield, MN · host Carleton · Bill Huyck Championship Course

The D-III national championship. Men: No. 1 Wartburg and No. 2 North Central (Ill.), which beat Wartburg at the Midwest Regional, lead 32 teams with No. 3 Johns Hopkins. Women: unanimous No. 1 NYU goes for back-to-back titles against No. 2 Williams, No. 3 Middlebury and No. 4 UW-La Crosse, with host Carleton in the field. · Women 6K 1:00 p.m. ET · Men 8K 2:00 p.m. ET
