Division I matchup of the week · Fri Nov 13

South Regional: Alabama and Florida, ranked in both races

NCAA D-I South Regional Cross Country Championships · Tallahassee, FL · host Florida State · Apalachee Regional Park

No. 4 Alabama and No. 8 Florida lead the men’s race with No. 24 Tennessee, and No. 4 Alabama and No. 5 Florida meet again in the women’s race at Apalachee Regional Park. The top two teams in each race earn automatic bids to the NCAA Championships. Women run 6K at 8:30 a.m. ET and men 10K at 9:30.
