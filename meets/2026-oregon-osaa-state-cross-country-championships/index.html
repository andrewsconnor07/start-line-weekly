Oregon: Summit boys and Crater girls, both No. 2 in the nation

Oregon OSAA State Cross Country Championships · Eugene, OR · Lane Community College

Two No. 2 teams in DyeStat’s national rankings race at Lane Community College: Summit’s boys and Crater’s girls. Lincoln’s Ellery Lincoln (No. 4 girls) and South Eugene’s Yosuke Shibata (No. 9 boys) are among the top individuals in the country.
