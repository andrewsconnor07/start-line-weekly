Division I matchup of the week · Fri Oct 9

Nuttycombe: New Mexico wins the men's titleFinal

Nuttycombe Wisconsin Invitational · Madison, WI · host Wisconsin · Thomas Zimmer Championship Cross Country Course

New Mexico won the men's title with 59 points to Colorado's 93, with No. 7 BYU third at 146; Colorado freshman Meshack Kipruto won in a course-record 22:30.1, 0.7 seconds ahead of New Mexico's Pasanjo Kitonga. West Virginia took the women's race with 66 points to Florida's 111 and Northern Arizona's 130, and Florida's Judy Chepkoech won in 19:19.5.
