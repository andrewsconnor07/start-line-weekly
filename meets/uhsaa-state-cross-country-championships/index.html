UHSAA State Cross Country ChampionshipsFinal

UHSAA State Cross Country Championships · Salt Lake City, UT · Sugar House Park

Final: Lehi won the 6A boys title with 61 points to No. 6 American Fork's 66 and No. 7 Riverton's 82, and Herriman's Trevin Lounsbury won the race in 15:08.08; No. 5 Lone Peak won the 6A girls with 30 points to American Fork's 86, and Farmington's Ellie Dorius won in 17:54.41. The other boys team champions were Maple Mountain (5A), Mountain View (4A), Ogden (3A), Grand County (2A) and Bryce Valley (1A), and the other girls champions were Viewmont (5A), Orem (4A), Canyon View (3A), North Summit (2A) and Panguitch (1A). Ogden's Avery Barton ran the fastest girls time of the meet, 17:49.88, to win 3A, and Northridge's Jeff Hazard won 5A boys in 15:06.53.
