Haverford Invitational · Haverford, PA · host Haverford · Fran Rizzo Cross Country Course

Final: No. 22 Haverford's men were second with 44 points behind Saint Joseph's (26) and ahead of Wilmington (73) and Jefferson (82). Unattached Mathew Bogdan won the men's 4-mile race in 19:23.7, with Villanova's Dan Watcke (19:43.9) the top collegian. Penn won the women's 5K with 27 points over Villanova (38), and Jefferson's Olivia Grahn won in 17:17.3.
