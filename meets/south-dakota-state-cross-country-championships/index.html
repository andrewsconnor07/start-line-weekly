South Dakota State Cross Country ChampionshipsFinal

South Dakota State Cross Country Championships · Rapid City, SD · Hart Ranch Camping Resort

Final: Harrisburg's Brianna Reilly repeated as Class AA girls champion in 17:06.5, leading a 1-2 with Hannah Genrich (17:20.5) as No. 18 Harrisburg beat Sioux Falls Lincoln for the team title, 41-71, with Spearfish's Peyton VanDeest fourth (17:36.2). Sioux Falls Washington's Cole Reilly won the Class AA boys race in 14:53.9 and led Washington past Sioux Falls Lincoln, 51-81, while Sioux Falls Christian swept the Class A team titles behind individual winners Sadie Pettengill (17:26.7) and Levi Vander Leest (15:50.5). Deubrook Area's Nora Olsen (18:27.3) and Mitchell Christian's Silas Holdeman (16:07.7) won the Class B races.
