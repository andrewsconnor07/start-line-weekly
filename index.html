Division I final · Sat Oct 31 · Stillwater, OK

Big 12: Colorado edges Oklahoma State, BYU women win

Colorado edged No. 2 Oklahoma State 36-38 for the men's title in Stillwater, with No. 5 BYU third at 91. Oklahoma State's Denis Kipngetich won the 8K in 23:05.7, with Colorado's Dominic Serem second in 23:06.9. In the women's race, No. 1 BYU won with 56 points to No. 3 West Virginia's 63, with No. 7 Oklahoma State third (116) and No. 10 Colorado fourth (117); BYU's Jane Hedengren won the 6K in 19:28.0.

Division I final · Fri Oct 30 · Columbia, MO

SEC: Alabama sweeps the men’s and women’s titles

Alabama won both SEC titles. The No. 4 Crimson Tide men scored 37 points to beat No. 7 Arkansas (76) and No. 9 Florida (82), with Florida's Kelvin Cheruiyot winning the 8K in 22:03.1. The women's team also won, scoring 48 to Florida's 80 with South Carolina third at 110, as Alabama's Caren Kiplagat took the 6K in 18:46.5, 1.4 seconds ahead of Arkansas' Isca Chelangat.

Division III final · Sat Oct 31 · Middlebury, VT

NESCAC: Williams holds off Middlebury, Amherst wins by one

Williams won the women’s title with 36 points, four ahead of host Middlebury (40), with Tufts third (94). Middlebury’s Audrey MacLean won the 6K in 20:54.4, ahead of teammate Annelise Jensen (21:14.1) and Williams’ Kate Tuttle (21:19.5). Amherst took the men’s title by one point, 66 to Williams’ 67, with Tufts third (70), Wesleyan fourth (83) and Middlebury fifth (116); Wesleyan’s Dylan Cote won the 8K in 24:31.1.

High school final · Sat Oct 31 · Colorado Springs, CO

Colorado state: Niwot sweeps the 5A titles

Niwot swept the 5A titles. The No. 4 boys won their fourth straight with 60 points, well clear of Arapahoe (143), while Northfield's Abdinasir Hassan won the individual race in 14:56.41 and set the 5A course record. The No. 7 girls won with 47 points over No. 9 Boulder (100) and Fairview (116), and Cherokee Trail's Madison Lange won the individual title in 17:27.99. Coronado's Oliver Horton won the 4A boys race in 14:40.52 as Summit took the team title (75), and Eaton's Delaney Reuter won her third straight 3A girls title in 17:22.02 as Berthoud won the team title (62).
