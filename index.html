Division I final · Fri Oct 16 · Terre Haute, IN

Pre-Nationals: BYU edges NC State 84-88

No. 2 BYU edged No. 1 NC State 84-88 on the LaVern Gibson course that hosts the NCAA Championships, with NC State's Hannah Gapes winning the women's race in 18:57.6; No. 7 Notre Dame was third (213). In the men's race, No. 8 Syracuse won with 115 ahead of No. 14 Villanova (127) and No. 15 Notre Dame (148), and Tulane's Silas Kiptanui won in 23:17.4.

Division III final · Sat Oct 17 · Waterford, CT

Connecticut College: MIT wins the men's showdown

Men's Blue race: No. 10 MIT won with 57 points, ahead of No. 16 Williams (120), No. 21 Wesleyan (173), No. 15 Tufts (196) and No. 29 Bates (197), and Wesleyan's Dylan Cote won in 23:52.8. Women's Blue race: No. 8 Middlebury beat No. 1 Williams 63-65, with No. 6 Tufts third (131), and Middlebury's Audrey Maclean won in 20:36.6. MIT also won the men's White race (52) and Williams the women's White race (25).

High school final · Sat Oct 17 · Mechanicsville, VA

VA XC Showcase: Christ School and Union Catholic win

Boys Showcase: No. 1 Christ School won with 26 points, ahead of No. 26 Calvert Hall (MD) (66) and Cardinal Gibbons and Cuthbertson (154 each), and South Eugene's Yosuke Shibata won in 14:53.7. Girls Showcase: No. 1 Union Catholic (NJ) scored 65 to beat Penn Charter (132), Blacksburg (154) and Marvin Ridge (183), with Georgetown Visitation eighth (283) and James Robinson tenth (322), and Peters Township's Kathryn Froelich won in 16:53.9.
