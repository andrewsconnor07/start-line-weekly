High school matchup of the week · Sat Oct 24 · Walnut, CA

Mt. SAC: Van Meerten returns to chase his own course record

Flagstaff (AZ) runner Yohanes Van Meerten, who set the Mt. SAC course record of 14:10.1 last year, runs the Division 1-2 boys team sweepstakes against California's best, including Mt. Carmel's Jaxon Northcutt (13:42.4 three-mile best) and Redondo Union, ranked fifth in California. In the girls race, Mira Costa, ranked fifth in California and last year's team champion, and Westlake's Sabina Cruz are among the favorites. Boys team sweepstakes at 9:43 a.m. PT, girls at 10:08 a.m.
