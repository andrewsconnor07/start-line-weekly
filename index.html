Division I matchup of the week · Fri Nov 13 · Albuquerque, NM

Mountain Regional: No. 1 New Mexico, No. 2 Colorado and No. 1 BYU

Women run 6K at 11:00 a.m. MT and men 10K at noon.

High school matchup of the week · Sat Nov 14 · Holmdel, NJ

Meet of Champions: No. 1 Union Catholic and No. 2 Leah Starkey

No. 1 Union Catholic’s girls, who scored a perfect 15 at last week’s Group meet, go for a fifth straight Meet of Champions title, with No. 19 Ridgewood also in the field. Ocean Township’s Leah Starkey, the No. 2 girl in the country, ran 16:45 at Holmdel last week. In the boys race, Union Catholic and No. 16 CBA meet again a week after Union Catholic beat CBA for the Non-Public A title.
