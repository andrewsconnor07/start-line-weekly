High school matchup of the week · Sat Oct 3 · Cary, NC

Great American: Christ School vs. Belen Jesuit

DyeStat's No. 1 team, Christ School (NC), won last year's boys Race of Champions; No. 6 Belen Jesuit (FL) won it in 2024. Belen's Marcelo Mantecon, DyeStat's No. 1 individual, goes for a third straight title against No. 6 Brooks Barbee of Christ School and No. 9 Drew Beroset. In the girls race, No. 3 Averi Lowen defends her title.
