Division I matchup of the week · Sat Oct 31 · Stillwater, OK

Big 12: BYU, Oklahoma State and Colorado meet for the title

Three top-5 men’s teams meet in Stillwater: No. 2 Oklahoma State, No. 3 Colorado and No. 5 BYU. On the women’s side, No. 1 BYU faces No. 3 West Virginia, No. 7 Oklahoma State and No. 10 Colorado. The women’s 6K starts at 10:00 a.m. CT and the men’s 8K at 11:00 a.m. CT, on ESPN.

Division III matchup of the week · Sat Oct 31 · Middlebury, VT

NESCAC: Middlebury, Williams and Tufts women race for the title

No. 2 Middlebury hosts No. 4 Williams and No. 7 Tufts. Middlebury beat Williams 63-65 at the Connecticut College Invitational two weeks ago, and Williams won the 2025 NESCAC women’s title, its sixth straight. The women’s 6K starts at 11:00 a.m. ET and the men’s 8K at noon ET.

High school matchup of the week · Sat Oct 31 · Colorado Springs, CO

Colorado state: Niwot, Boulder and ThunderRidge meet a national-level field

Defending 5A champion Niwot brings DyeStat’s No. 4 boys team and No. 7 girls team to Colorado Springs, with No. 8 Boulder and No. 9 ThunderRidge in the girls race. Northfield’s Abdi Hassan, No. 2 in DyeStat’s individual rankings, Coronado’s Oliver Horton (No. 10) and Eaton’s Delaney Reuter (No. 9 girls) are all entered. No. 29 Mountain Vista looks to pull an upset in the boys race with its star-studded duo of Benjamin Adams and Riley Kiziah. The 5A girls race starts at 1:00 p.m. MT and the 5A boys at 2:20 p.m.
