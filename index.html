Division I final · Fri Oct 9 · Madison, WI

Nuttycombe: New Mexico wins the men's title

New Mexico won the men's title with 59 points to Colorado's 93, with No. 7 BYU third at 146; Colorado freshman Meshack Kipruto won in 22:30.1, 0.7 seconds ahead of New Mexico's Pasanjo Kitonga. No. 2 Iowa State was 19th at 514, with its first runner 67th. West Virginia took the women's race with 66 points to Florida's 111 and Northern Arizona's 130, and Florida's Judy Chepkoech won in 19:19.5, 0.1 ahead of Iowa State's Betty Kipkore.

High school matchup of the week · Sat Oct 10 · Fresno, CA

Clovis: Crater leads a loaded Woodward Park field

DyeStat No. 2 Crater (OR) girls, fresh off their Woodbridge win, headline the Saturday championship race against No. 10 St. Francis (Sacramento) and No. 12 Buchanan. On the boys side, No. 10 Crater takes on No. 16 Mountain View (CA). In the girls race, No. 22 Brynn Davenport of Crater and No. 23 Addyson Johns of Santiago (Corona), second and third at Woodbridge, meet again.
