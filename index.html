Division I matchup of the week · Sat Nov 21 · Terre Haute, IN

NCAA Championships: Unanimous No. 1 New Mexico chases a first title

New Mexico, second a year ago, is the unanimous No. 1 after beating No. 2 Colorado and No. 6 BYU at the Mountain Regional, with defending champion No. 3 Oklahoma State and No. 4 Alabama next. In the women’s race, No. 1 BYU and defending champion No. 2 NC State, who have won the last six titles between them, face No. 3 West Virginia and No. 4 Alabama.
