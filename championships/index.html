Virginia VISAA State Cross Country ChampionshipsFinal

Earlysville, VA · Panorama Farms

Final: Division I: Norfolk Academy won the boys title with 99 points over St. Stephen’s and St. Agnes (107) as Bishop Ireton’s Jason Day won in 15:36.4, and The Potomac School beat Collegiate 56-70 for the girls title as Collegiate’s Virginia Harris won in 18:43.6.
